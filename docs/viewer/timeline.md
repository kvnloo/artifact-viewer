# Timeline

The viewer is the cumulation of the conversation: the ask, the piece, the judgment, the next piece.

In public, that cumulation is the fork issue. The body is the decision. The proposed order, when the issue has one, is pulled out above the body. Comments stay on GitHub.

In private, the same shape is a Hermes session. The [local indexer](/guide/local-indexer) walks `state.db`, attaches `MEDIA:` paths and replied-to Telegram photos, and can iframe a live preview when the port is up.

Rounds come from filenames (`r10`) and from judge notes. Scores come from the words in the reply. Neither is a separate database.
